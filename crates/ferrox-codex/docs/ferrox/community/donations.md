# 💖 Support & Donations

First of all, **thank you** for using and considering supporting **Ferrox Framework**! 🚀

Ferrox is a progressive, 100% open-source enterprise framework for Rust, built with love and dedicated to high-performance, safe, and modern backend engineering.

---

## 💸 Why We Need Your Support

Maintaining a complete ecosystem of **45 modular crates**, managing domain infrastructure (`ferrox-rust.dev`), running automated integration test pipelines, conducting benchmark suites, and maintaining multi-language documentation involves ongoing server, domain, and operational costs.

Every donation — no matter how small — directly helps us:
- ⚡ **Accelerate Feature Development:** Dedicated time to implement new enterprise capabilities and stabilize core crates.
- 🛡️ **Enhance Security & Resilience:** Continuously test and refine zero-trust authentication, rate-limiters, and circuit breakers.
- 🌐 **Cover Operational Costs:** Maintain domain registration, test servers, benchmark infrastructure, and official email services (`info@ferrox-rust.dev`).
- 🤝 **Keep Ferrox Free & Open:** Ensure Ferrox remains 100% free, community-driven, and open-source under `MIT OR Apache-2.0`.
